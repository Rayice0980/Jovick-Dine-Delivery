Frequently asked
questions.

A few helpful answers before you place an order. If you need something else, our contact page is a good place to start.

Where is Jovick Dine Delivery based?

Our head office is in Abuja, Federal Capital Territory, Nigeria. Specific delivery coverage should be confirmed before ordering.

Which areas in Abuja do you deliver to?

Coverage depends on the operating delivery zones. Wuse, Garki, Maitama, Gwarinpa and other areas should only be advertised as active service zones once confirmed by the business.

How much does delivery cost?

Delivery charges can vary by distance and operating conditions. The website should show the confirmed fee for your address before an order is finalised.

Can I pay online?

This website is currently a front-end demonstration and does not process payments. A secure payment provider and backend must be connected before online payment is available.

Can I change or cancel an order?

Once a real ordering service is active, contact the team as soon as possible. A clear cancellation policy should be published before taking live orders.

Do you cater for allergies or dietary needs?

Please contact the business to confirm ingredients and cross-contamination practices. Never assume an item is allergen-free without direct confirmation.

Is the menu always available?

Some dishes may sell out or vary by day. A live menu should reflect actual availability once the ordering system is connected.
