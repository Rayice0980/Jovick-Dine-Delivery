Our story

Rooted in Nigerian food culture. Made for modern Abuja life.

Some days call for a plate of smoky jollof. Other days, it’s soup and swallow, a quick lunch between meetings, or a small treat just because. Jovick is here for those everyday moments.

Our aim is to make discovering and ordering your favourite meals feel easy, clear and dependable—from browsing the menu to knowing what happens next.

We’re building a local food experience with a warm welcome, straightforward information and respect for the people who make every meal possible.
